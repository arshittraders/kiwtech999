Last updated: 28 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arshit Traders ("Kiwtech", "we", "us", "our"), operating the website kiwtech.in and the Kiwtech Meesho Research Tool browser extension (the "Service"), collects, uses, and protects your information. By using our website or Service, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & purchase details: Name, email address, and state (collected for GST invoicing) at checkout.

  • Usage data: Basic technical logs (such as activation status and error logs) to operate and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver your license key and software download link via email.

3. Sharing With Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited information with trusted service providers only as needed to run the Service:
