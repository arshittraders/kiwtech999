Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 28 May 2026

Kiwtech, operated by Arshit Traders, sells a digital software product (the Kiwtech Meesho Research Tool browser extension). There is no physical shipment involved.

1. Delivery Method

After your payment is successfully completed via Razorpay, your order is delivered electronically to the email address you provided at checkout. The delivery includes:

  • Your unique license key.
  • A download link for the extension.
  • Installation instructions and a GST invoice.

2. Delivery Time

Delivery is typically instant and occurs within a few minutes of successful payment. In rare cases it may take up to 24 hours.

3. Not Received Your Order?

Please check your email Spam / Promotions folder first. If you still have not received your license within 24 hours, contact us with your payment ID and we will resend it promptly.

4. No Shipping Charges

As the product is digital, there are no shipping or delivery charges. The price shown at checkout (plus applicable GST) is the total amount payable.
