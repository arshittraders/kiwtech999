Kiwtech is a digital software product. Upon successful payment, a license key and software download link are delivered instantly to your email. Because the product is delivered digitally and access is granted immediately, sales are generally considered final.

We want you to have a fair experience. A refund may be granted in the following cases:

Refund requests based on a change of mind, lack of usage, or failure to achieve specific business results (such as increased sales or ranking) are not eligible.

4. How to Request a Refund

Contact us within 7 days of purchase via email or WhatsApp with your payment ID, registered email, and a description of the issue. We will review and respond promptly.

5. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method via Razorpay, typically within 5–7 business days. The time for the amount to reflect depends on your bank or payment provider.

6. Cancellation

Our plans do not auto-renew. You are not charged automatically, so there is nothing to cancel — simply do not repurchase if you do not wish to continue. Your access will expire at the end of the purchased period.
